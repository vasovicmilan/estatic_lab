# Price on request

For products whose price is too volatile to show automatically, the customer can't buy directly - they send an inquiry, and the admin manually creates the order with the agreed price.

## 1. Home page

We start on the site's home page.

![otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/cena-na-upit/01-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Shop

In the menu we click “Prodavnica” - the products that can be ordered.

![nav-prodavnica](../../screenshots/cena-na-upit/02-nav-prodavnica.png)

## 3. A product with no price

In the product list we click the product and see “Price on request” instead of a price - there is no add-to-cart button.

![otvaranje-proizvoda-na-upit](../../screenshots/cena-na-upit/03-otvaranje-proizvoda-na-upit.png)

## 4. Go to the contact form

We click “Kontaktirajte nas za cenu” and arrive at the contact form, already pre-filled with the product name.

![prelazak-na-kontakt-formu](../../screenshots/cena-na-upit/04-prelazak-na-kontakt-formu.png)

## 5. Fill in the inquiry

The customer enters their details and a message.

![popunjavanje-upita](../../screenshots/cena-na-upit/05-popunjavanje-upita.png)

## 6. Send the inquiry

The customer sends the inquiry - this doesn't create any order yet, just a message for the admin.

![slanje-upita](../../screenshots/cena-na-upit/06-slanje-upita.png)

## 7. Home page

The administrator starts on the site's home page.

![admin-otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/cena-na-upit/07-admin-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 8. Log in

We click “Prijava”.

![admin-login-klik-prijava](../../screenshots/cena-na-upit/08-admin-login-klik-prijava.png)

## 9. Admin logs in

The admin logs in to handle the inquiry.

![admin-prijava](../../screenshots/cena-na-upit/09-admin-prijava.png)

## 10. Open the account menu

The admin clicks the dropdown with their name in the top right corner.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni](../../screenshots/cena-na-upit/10-admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni.png)

## 11. Enter the admin panel

The admin chooses “Admin panel” from the menu, which opens the admin part of the site.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel](../../screenshots/cena-na-upit/11-admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel.png)

## 12. Open the "Store" menu

The admin clicks the "Store" group.

![admin-porudzbine-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/cena-na-upit/12-admin-porudzbine-otvara-meni.png)

## 13. Open orders

The admin picks "Orders" from the menu.

![admin-porudzbine-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/cena-na-upit/13-admin-porudzbine-bira-stavku.png)

## 14. New manual order

The admin clicks "New order (manual)" - a form outside the normal checkout, made for exactly this case.

![admin-porudzbine-klik-na-dodaj](../../screenshots/cena-na-upit/14-admin-porudzbine-klik-na-dodaj.png)

## 15. Select the product

The admin selects the product and its variant - the list clearly shows the price is "on request".

![admin-bira-proizvod](../../screenshots/cena-na-upit/15-admin-bira-proizvod.png)

## 16. Enter contact details and price

The admin enters the customer's details and, critically, a hand-entered price - without this, an order for this product couldn't be created at all.

![admin-unosi-kontakt-i-cenu](../../screenshots/cena-na-upit/16-admin-unosi-kontakt-i-cenu.png)

## 17. Create the order

The admin creates the order - this immediately creates a real order, with no further confirmation step from the customer.

![admin-kreira-porudzbinu](../../screenshots/cena-na-upit/17-admin-kreira-porudzbinu.png)
