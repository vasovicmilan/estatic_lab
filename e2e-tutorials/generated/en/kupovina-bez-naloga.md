# Buying without an account (guest)

A customer buys with no registration at all: picks a product in the shop, enters their details at checkout and the system creates an account in the background. Two purchases are shown (the second with a coupon) - each with a new guest, so the automatic account creation happens both times.

<video src="../../videos/kupovina-bez-naloga/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

We start on the site's home page.

![otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/01-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Shop

In the menu we click “Prodavnica” - the products that can be ordered.

![nav-prodavnica](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/02-nav-prodavnica.png)

## 3. Choosing the first product

In the product list we click the first product.

![otvaranje-prvog-proizvoda](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/03-otvaranje-prvog-proizvoda.png)

## 4. Add to cart

We click “Dodaj u korpu” - the system opens the cart straight away.

![dodavanje-u-korpu-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/04-dodavanje-u-korpu-1.png)

## 5. Go to checkout

In the cart we click “Nastavi na naplatu”. We are not logged in - the system points that out and explains that an account will be created automatically.

![prelazak-na-naplatu-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/05-prelazak-na-naplatu-1.png)

## 6. Guest details

Nothing is pre-filled - we type the first name, last name, email, phone and delivery address.

![unos-podataka-kao-gost-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/06-unos-podataka-kao-gost-1.png)

## 7. Confirm the order

We click “Potvrdi porudžbinu”.

![potvrda-porudzbine-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/07-potvrda-porudzbine-1.png)

## 8. Account created automatically

In the background the system has created a real user account for this email - the customer did not have to ask for it.

![nalog-automatski-kreiran-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/08-nalog-automatski-kreiran-1.png)

## 9. Confirm via the link

The link from the confirmation email finalizes the order (simulated here by opening the link directly).

![email-potvrde-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/09-email-potvrde-1.png)

## 10. Shop

In the menu we click “Prodavnica” - the products that can be ordered.

![nav-prodavnica](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/10-nav-prodavnica.png)

## 11. Choosing the second product

For the second purchase we click “Prodavnica” again and pick the second product from the list.

![otvaranje-drugog-proizvoda](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/11-otvaranje-drugog-proizvoda.png)

## 12. Add to cart

We click “Dodaj u korpu” - the system opens the cart.

![dodavanje-u-korpu-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/12-dodavanje-u-korpu-2.png)

## 13. Go to checkout

In the cart we click “Nastavi na naplatu”.

![prelazak-na-naplatu-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/13-prelazak-na-naplatu-2.png)

## 14. Second guest's details

A second guest with a different email - again we type all the details from scratch.

![unos-podataka-kao-gost-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/14-unos-podataka-kao-gost-2.png)

## 15. Apply the coupon

We type the coupon code and click the apply button - the reduced amount shows immediately.

![primena-kupona-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/15-primena-kupona-2.png)

## 16. Confirm the order

We click “Potvrdi porudžbinu”.

![potvrda-porudzbine-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/16-potvrda-porudzbine-2.png)

## 17. Account created automatically

For the second email the system creates an account on its own too.

![nalog-automatski-kreiran-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/17-nalog-automatski-kreiran-2.png)

## 18. Confirm via the link

The email link finalizes the second order as well.

![email-potvrde-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/18-email-potvrde-2.png)
