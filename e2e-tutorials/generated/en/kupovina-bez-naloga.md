# Checkout without an account (guest)

A customer never has to register - filling in checkout details is enough, and an account gets created automatically behind the scenes. Shown here twice: once without a coupon, once with one, with two different customers.

<video src="../../videos/kupovina-bez-naloga/merged.en.burned.webm" controls width="720"></video>

## 1. Open the first product

The customer (not logged in) opens a product and adds it to the cart.

![otvaranje-prvog-proizvoda](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/01-otvaranje-prvog-proizvoda.png)

## 2. Proceed to checkout

On the checkout page, the system clearly explains an account will be created automatically if the customer doesn't have one.

![prelazak-na-naplatu-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/02-prelazak-na-naplatu-1.png)

## 3. Enter details as a guest

The customer enters everything from scratch - name, email, phone, address - nothing is pre-filled.

![unos-podataka-kao-gost-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/03-unos-podataka-kao-gost-1.png)

## 4. Confirm the order

The customer confirms the order with no coupon at all.

![potvrda-porudzbine-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/04-potvrda-porudzbine-1.png)

## 5. The account was created automatically

A real account was just created behind the scenes for this email - the customer never had to ask for it.

![nalog-automatski-kreiran-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/05-nalog-automatski-kreiran-1.png)

## 6. Confirmation email

The customer finalizes the first order by clicking the link from the email.

![email-potvrde-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/06-email-potvrde-1.png)

## 7. Open the second product

A new, completely different customer opens a second product - again without logging in.

![otvaranje-drugog-proizvoda](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/07-otvaranje-drugog-proizvoda.png)

## 8. Proceed to checkout

The same automatic-account message appears again - this really is a new, first-time customer.

![prelazak-na-naplatu-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/08-prelazak-na-naplatu-2.png)

## 9. Enter details as a guest

The customer enters everything from scratch again.

![unos-podataka-kao-gost-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/09-unos-podataka-kao-gost-2.png)

## 10. Apply the coupon

Unlike the first purchase, this customer has a coupon code and applies it.

![primena-kupona-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga/10-primena-kupona-2.png)

## 11. Confirm the order

The customer confirms the order with the coupon discount applied.

The same thing happens for this customer too - completely independent of the first one.
