# Booking an appointment by hand (phone)

An administrator books an appointment for a client who phoned: service, variant, therapist, time and details. Recorded on a phone (390x844).

<video src="../../videos/rucno-zakazivanje-mobilni/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

The administrator starts on the site's home page.

![admin-otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/rucno-zakazivanje-mobilni/01-admin-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button in the top right corner. We tap it.

![admin-login-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/rucno-zakazivanje-mobilni/02-admin-login-otvara-meni.png)

## 3. Log in

We tap “Prijava”.

![admin-login-klik-prijava](../../screenshots/rucno-zakazivanje-mobilni/03-admin-login-klik-prijava.png)

## 4. Admin logs in

The administrator types the email and password and logs in.

![admin-prijava](../../screenshots/rucno-zakazivanje-mobilni/04-admin-prijava.png)

## 5. Menu

On a phone the admin opens the main menu with the three-line button.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni](../../screenshots/rucno-zakazivanje-mobilni/05-admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni.png)

## 6. Admin panel

In the “Admin” section they tap “Admin panel”.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel](../../screenshots/rucno-zakazivanje-mobilni/06-admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel.png)

## 7. “Zakazivanje” menu

We open the “Zakazivanje” group.

![admin-termini-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/rucno-zakazivanje-mobilni/07-admin-termini-otvara-meni.png)

## 8. Termini

We choose “Termini”.

![admin-termini-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/rucno-zakazivanje-mobilni/08-admin-termini-bira-stavku.png)

## 9. New appointment (manual)

We tap “Novi termin (ručno)”. It is used when a client phones in or walks in without booking.

![admin-termini-klik-na-dodaj](../../screenshots/rucno-zakazivanje-mobilni/09-admin-termini-klik-na-dodaj.png)

## 10. Service and variant

We choose the service, then the variant. Variants only appear once a service is chosen.

![rucno-usluga](../../screenshots/rucno-zakazivanje-mobilni/10-rucno-usluga.png)

## 11. Therapist

We choose the therapist who performs the service. It can be left empty, and the appointment stays unassigned.

![rucno-terapeut](../../screenshots/rucno-zakazivanje-mobilni/11-rucno-terapeut.png)

## 12. Date and time

We enter the date and start time.

![rucno-vreme](../../screenshots/rucno-zakazivanje-mobilni/12-rucno-vreme.png)

## 13. Client details

We enter the client's details. For an existing user we can pick them from the list instead of typing.

![rucno-klijent](../../screenshots/rucno-zakazivanje-mobilni/13-rucno-klijent.png)

## 14. Note

The note is optional; here we just record how the appointment was booked.

![rucno-napomena](../../screenshots/rucno-zakazivanje-mobilni/14-rucno-napomena.png)

## 15. Create the appointment

We tap “Kreiraj termin”. A manual appointment is not bound by the one-hour lead time. We can also set the price by hand, for example for a giveaway.

![rucno-cuvanje](../../screenshots/rucno-zakazivanje-mobilni/15-rucno-cuvanje.png)
