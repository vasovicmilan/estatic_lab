# Employee managing their own appointments (phone)

An employee has their own dedicated account area for their appointments - confirming, completing, and reviewing earned commission - entirely separate from the admin panel.

<video src="../../videos/zaposleni-upravlja-terminima-mobilni/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

We start on the site's home page.

![otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/zaposleni-upravlja-terminima-mobilni/01-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button in the top right corner. We tap it.

![login-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/zaposleni-upravlja-terminima-mobilni/02-login-otvara-meni.png)

## 3. Log in

In the menu we tap “Prijava”.

![login-klik-prijava](../../screenshots/zaposleni-upravlja-terminima-mobilni/03-login-klik-prijava.png)

## 4. Enter your login details

The employee enters the email and password and logs in.

![unos-prijave](../../screenshots/zaposleni-upravlja-terminima-mobilni/04-unos-prijave.png)

## 5. Account menu

After logging in we open the menu again. At the bottom is a section with the employee's name and account links - the page scrolls to it.

![portal-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/zaposleni-upravlja-terminima-mobilni/05-portal-otvara-meni.png)

## 6. My calendar

We tap “Moj kalendar” and enter the employee's account.

![portal-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/zaposleni-upravlja-terminima-mobilni/06-portal-bira-stavku.png)

## 7. My appointments

On the bottom bar we tap “Termini” and see the appointments assigned to this employee.

![pregled-mojih-termina](../../screenshots/zaposleni-upravlja-terminima-mobilni/07-pregled-mojih-termina.png)

## 8. Appointment details

Clicking the appointment opens its details, with options to confirm and complete it.

![otvaranje-detalja-termina](../../screenshots/zaposleni-upravlja-terminima-mobilni/08-otvaranje-detalja-termina.png)

## 9. Confirm the appointment

The employee confirms the appointment - the customer now has a firm, confirmed slot.

![potvrda-termina](../../screenshots/zaposleni-upravlja-terminima-mobilni/09-potvrda-termina.png)

## 10. Complete the appointment

Once the service has been delivered, the employee marks it completed - this is also when their commission gets recorded.

![zavrsavanje-termina](../../screenshots/zaposleni-upravlja-terminima-mobilni/10-zavrsavanje-termina.png)

## 11. Earned commission

On the “Provizija” tab the employee sees the commission the appointment earned.

![pregled-provizije](../../screenshots/zaposleni-upravlja-terminima-mobilni/11-pregled-provizije.png)
