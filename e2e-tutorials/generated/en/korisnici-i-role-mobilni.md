# Users and roles (phone)

An administrator finds a user by email in “Korisnici” and looks at the roles and permissions in “Role”. Recorded on a phone (390x844).

<video src="../../videos/korisnici-i-role-mobilni/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

The administrator starts on the site's home page.

![admin-otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/korisnici-i-role-mobilni/01-admin-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button in the top right corner. We tap it.

![admin-login-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/korisnici-i-role-mobilni/02-admin-login-otvara-meni.png)

## 3. Log in

We tap “Prijava”.

![admin-login-klik-prijava](../../screenshots/korisnici-i-role-mobilni/03-admin-login-klik-prijava.png)

## 4. Admin logs in

The administrator types the email and password and logs in.

![admin-prijava](../../screenshots/korisnici-i-role-mobilni/04-admin-prijava.png)

## 5. Menu

On a phone the admin opens the main menu with the three-line button.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni](../../screenshots/korisnici-i-role-mobilni/05-admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni.png)

## 6. Admin panel

In the “Admin” section they tap “Admin panel”.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel](../../screenshots/korisnici-i-role-mobilni/06-admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel.png)

## 7. “Tim” menu

We open the “Tim” group.

![admin-korisnici-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/korisnici-i-role-mobilni/07-admin-korisnici-otvara-meni.png)

## 8. Korisnici

We choose “Korisnici”.

![admin-korisnici-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/korisnici-i-role-mobilni/08-admin-korisnici-bira-stavku.png)

## 9. Find the user

We find the user by email or name.

![admin-korisnici-pretraga](../../screenshots/korisnici-i-role-mobilni/09-admin-korisnici-pretraga.png)

## 10. User details

We open the user's account.

![admin-korisnici-otvaranje-rezultata](../../screenshots/korisnici-i-role-mobilni/10-admin-korisnici-otvaranje-rezultata.png)

## 11. The user's account

The account data, role and activity are visible. From here an account can also be deactivated.

![korisnik-detalji](../../screenshots/korisnici-i-role-mobilni/11-korisnik-detalji.png)

## 12. “Sistem” menu

We open the “Sistem” group.

![admin-role-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/korisnici-i-role-mobilni/12-admin-role-otvara-meni.png)

## 13. Role

We choose “Role”.

![admin-role-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/korisnici-i-role-mobilni/13-admin-role-bira-stavku.png)

## 14. Roles and permissions

A role decides what each person may do: administrator, employee, partner and user have different permissions.

![role-pregled](../../screenshots/korisnici-i-role-mobilni/14-role-pregled.png)
