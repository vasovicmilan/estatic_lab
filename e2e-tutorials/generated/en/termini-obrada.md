# Handling an appointment: confirm, reassign, complete

An administrator confirms a booked appointment, hands it to another therapist and marks it as completed.

<video src="../../videos/termini-obrada/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

The administrator starts on the site's home page.

![admin-otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/termini-obrada/01-admin-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Log in

We click “Prijava”.

![admin-login-klik-prijava](../../screenshots/termini-obrada/02-admin-login-klik-prijava.png)

## 3. Admin logs in

The administrator types the email and password and logs in.

![admin-prijava](../../screenshots/termini-obrada/03-admin-prijava.png)

## 4. Open the account menu

The admin clicks the dropdown with their name in the top right corner.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni](../../screenshots/termini-obrada/04-admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni.png)

## 5. Enter the admin panel

The admin chooses “Admin panel” from the menu, which opens the admin part of the site.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel](../../screenshots/termini-obrada/05-admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel.png)

## 6. “Zakazivanje” menu

We open the “Zakazivanje” group.

![admin-termini-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/termini-obrada/06-admin-termini-otvara-meni.png)

## 7. Termini

We choose “Termini”.

![admin-termini-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/termini-obrada/07-admin-termini-bira-stavku.png)

## 8. Search appointments

We look the appointment up by the client's first or last name.

![admin-termini-pretraga](../../screenshots/termini-obrada/08-admin-termini-pretraga.png)

## 9. Appointment details

We open the appointment details.

![admin-termini-otvaranje-rezultata](../../screenshots/termini-obrada/09-admin-termini-otvaranje-rezultata.png)

## 10. A new appointment awaits confirmation

An appointment the client booked is “pending”. Until we confirm it, it is not final.

![termin-pregled](../../screenshots/termini-obrada/10-termin-pregled.png)

## 11. Confirm the appointment

We click “Potvrdi termin” and confirm in the dialog. The client is notified.

![termin-potvrda](../../screenshots/termini-obrada/11-termin-potvrda.png)

## 12. A different therapist

If needed, we hand the appointment to another therapist. The list only offers those who are free and on shift at that time.

![termin-preraspodela](../../screenshots/termini-obrada/12-termin-preraspodela.png)

## 13. Complete the appointment

When the treatment is done, we click “Označi kao završen”. That is when the employee's commission is calculated and, if paid by a package, one session is spent.

![termin-zavrsetak](../../screenshots/termini-obrada/13-termin-zavrsetak.png)
