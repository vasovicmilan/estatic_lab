# Claiming an account after a guest purchase (phone)

A guest who booked or bought without an account opens the email link, chooses a password, claims the account and logs in. Recorded on a phone (390x844).

<video src="../../videos/preuzimanje-naloga-mobilni/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. The link from the email

Someone who booked or bought without an account gets an email with a “Preuzmite vaš nalog” link. We open it.

![link-iz-mejla](../../screenshots/preuzimanje-naloga-mobilni/01-link-iz-mejla.png)

## 2. Choose a password

They only need to choose a password (at least 8 characters): the name and email are already in the account.

![nova-lozinka](../../screenshots/preuzimanje-naloga-mobilni/02-nova-lozinka.png)

## 3. Claim the account

We tap “Preuzmi nalog”. Opening the email link also confirms the address, so the account is active at once, and the earlier appointments and orders stay in it.

![preuzimanje-cuvanje](../../screenshots/preuzimanje-naloga-mobilni/03-preuzimanje-cuvanje.png)

## 4. Log in

We log in with the new password.

![prijava-novom-lozinkom](../../screenshots/preuzimanje-naloga-mobilni/04-prijava-novom-lozinkom.png)
