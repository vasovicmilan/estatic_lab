# Site settings - booking policy and currency (phone)

An administrator changes the gap between appointments, the cancellation deadline and the currency display in “Podešavanja sajta”, with no code change and no restart. Recorded on a phone (390x844).

<video src="../../videos/podesavanja-sajta-mobilni/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

The administrator starts on the site's home page.

![admin-otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/podesavanja-sajta-mobilni/01-admin-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button in the top right corner. We tap it.

![admin-login-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/podesavanja-sajta-mobilni/02-admin-login-otvara-meni.png)

## 3. Log in

We tap “Prijava”.

![admin-login-klik-prijava](../../screenshots/podesavanja-sajta-mobilni/03-admin-login-klik-prijava.png)

## 4. Admin logs in

The administrator types the email and password and logs in.

![admin-prijava](../../screenshots/podesavanja-sajta-mobilni/04-admin-prijava.png)

## 5. Menu

On a phone the admin opens the main menu with the three-line button.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni](../../screenshots/podesavanja-sajta-mobilni/05-admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni.png)

## 6. Admin panel

In the “Admin” section they tap “Admin panel”.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel](../../screenshots/podesavanja-sajta-mobilni/06-admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel.png)

## 7. “Sadržaj i marketing” menu

We open the “Sadržaj i marketing” group.

![admin-sajt-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/podesavanja-sajta-mobilni/07-admin-sajt-otvara-meni.png)

## 8. Podešavanja sajta

We choose “Podešavanja sajta”.

![admin-sajt-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/podesavanja-sajta-mobilni/08-admin-sajt-bira-stavku.png)

## 9. Gap between appointments

The booking policy is data, not code. We change the gap between appointments here, e.g. to 45 minutes.

![sajt-politika-zakazivanja](../../screenshots/podesavanja-sajta-mobilni/09-sajt-politika-zakazivanja.png)

## 10. Cancellation deadline

The deadline until which a client may cancel their own appointment, e.g. 12 hours ahead.

![sajt-otkazivanje](../../screenshots/podesavanja-sajta-mobilni/10-sajt-otkazivanje.png)

## 11. Currency display

The currency label changes only how a price is displayed, not the amounts in the database.

![sajt-valuta](../../screenshots/podesavanja-sajta-mobilni/11-sajt-valuta.png)

## 12. Save

We save. The change is effective at once, with no server restart.

![sajt-cuvanje](../../screenshots/podesavanja-sajta-mobilni/12-sajt-cuvanje.png)
