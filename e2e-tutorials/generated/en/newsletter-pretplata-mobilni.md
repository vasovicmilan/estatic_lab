# Newsletter - sign-up and subscriber list (phone)

A visitor subscribes through the footer form (email, interest, consent), and an administrator sees the subscriber in “Newsletter”. Recorded on a phone (390x844).

<video src="../../videos/newsletter-pretplata-mobilni/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

We start on the site's home page.

![otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/newsletter-pretplata-mobilni/01-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Newsletter sign-up

At the bottom of every page there is a newsletter sign-up form.

![newsletter-forma](../../screenshots/newsletter-pretplata-mobilni/02-newsletter-forma.png)

## 3. Email address

The visitor types their email.

![newsletter-email](../../screenshots/newsletter-pretplata-mobilni/03-newsletter-email.png)

## 4. Interests

They choose what interests them and only get those messages.

![newsletter-interesovanje](../../screenshots/newsletter-pretplata-mobilni/04-newsletter-interesovanje.png)

## 5. Consent

Consent has to be ticked explicitly; without it sign-up is impossible, and the time of consent is stored as proof.

![newsletter-pristanak](../../screenshots/newsletter-pretplata-mobilni/05-newsletter-pristanak.png)

## 6. Subscribe

We tap “Prijavi se”. The page confirms the sign-up and a welcome message follows.

![newsletter-slanje](../../screenshots/newsletter-pretplata-mobilni/06-newsletter-slanje.png)

## 7. Home page

The administrator starts on the site's home page.

![admin-otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/newsletter-pretplata-mobilni/07-admin-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 8. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button in the top right corner. We tap it.

![admin-login-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/newsletter-pretplata-mobilni/08-admin-login-otvara-meni.png)

## 9. Log in

We tap “Prijava”.

![admin-login-klik-prijava](../../screenshots/newsletter-pretplata-mobilni/09-admin-login-klik-prijava.png)

## 10. Admin logs in

The administrator logs in.

![admin-prijava](../../screenshots/newsletter-pretplata-mobilni/10-admin-prijava.png)

## 11. Menu

On a phone the admin opens the main menu with the three-line button.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni](../../screenshots/newsletter-pretplata-mobilni/11-admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni.png)

## 12. Admin panel

In the “Admin” section they tap “Admin panel”.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel](../../screenshots/newsletter-pretplata-mobilni/12-admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel.png)

## 13. “Content and marketing” menu

We open the “Sadržaj i marketing” group.

![admin-newsletter-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/newsletter-pretplata-mobilni/13-admin-newsletter-otvara-meni.png)

## 14. Newsletter

We choose “Newsletter” - the subscriber list.

![admin-newsletter-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/newsletter-pretplata-mobilni/14-admin-newsletter-bira-stavku.png)

## 15. Find the subscriber

We find the subscriber by email.

![admin-newsletter-pretraga](../../screenshots/newsletter-pretplata-mobilni/15-admin-newsletter-pretraga.png)

## 16. Subscriber details

We open the subscriber.

![admin-newsletter-otvaranje-rezultata](../../screenshots/newsletter-pretplata-mobilni/16-admin-newsletter-otvaranje-rezultata.png)

## 17. Subscriber recorded

The new sign-up is there at once, with status and time. For the visitor, unsubscribing is one click from every email.

![admin-newsletter-pregled](../../screenshots/newsletter-pretplata-mobilni/17-admin-newsletter-pregled.png)
