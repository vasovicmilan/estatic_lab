# Experts - adding a team member (phone)

An administrator adds a team member in “Eksperti”, who appears on the public “Naš tim” page at once. Recorded on a phone (390x844).

<video src="../../videos/eksperti-dodavanje-mobilni/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

The administrator starts on the site's home page.

![admin-otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje-mobilni/01-admin-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button in the top right corner. We tap it.

![admin-login-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje-mobilni/02-admin-login-otvara-meni.png)

## 3. Log in

We tap “Prijava”.

![admin-login-klik-prijava](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje-mobilni/03-admin-login-klik-prijava.png)

## 4. Admin logs in

The administrator types the email and password and logs in.

![admin-prijava](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje-mobilni/04-admin-prijava.png)

## 5. Menu

On a phone the admin opens the main menu with the three-line button.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje-mobilni/05-admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni.png)

## 6. Admin panel

In the “Admin” section they tap “Admin panel”.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje-mobilni/06-admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel.png)

## 7. “Tim” menu

We open the “Tim” group.

![admin-eksperti-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje-mobilni/07-admin-eksperti-otvara-meni.png)

## 8. Eksperti

We choose “Eksperti”.

![admin-eksperti-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje-mobilni/08-admin-eksperti-bira-stavku.png)

## 9. New expert

We tap “Novi ekspert”. An expert is a team member shown on the public “Naš tim” page; it is not the same as an employee account.

![admin-eksperti-klik-na-dodaj](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje-mobilni/09-admin-eksperti-klik-na-dodaj.png)

## 10. First and last name

We type the first and last name.

![ekspert-ime](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje-mobilni/10-ekspert-ime.png)

## 11. Title and bio

The title and short bio are shown on the card.

![ekspert-titula](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje-mobilni/11-ekspert-titula.png)

## 12. Photo

The photo is required, with alt text.

![ekspert-slika](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje-mobilni/12-ekspert-slika.png)
