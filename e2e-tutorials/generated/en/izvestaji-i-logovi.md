# Business reports and logs

An administrator looks at the business reports and the system logs, from the admin menu.

<video src="../../videos/izvestaji-i-logovi/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

## 1. Home page

The administrator starts on the site's home page.

![admin-otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi/01-admin-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Log in

We click “Prijava”.

![admin-login-klik-prijava](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi/02-admin-login-klik-prijava.png)

## 3. Admin logs in

The administrator types the email and password and logs in.

![admin-prijava](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi/03-admin-prijava.png)

## 4. Open the account menu

The admin clicks the dropdown with their name in the top right corner.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi/04-admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni.png)

## 5. Enter the admin panel

The admin chooses “Admin panel” from the menu, which opens the admin part of the site.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi/05-admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel.png)

## 6. “Poslovni izveštaji” menu

We open the “Poslovni izveštaji” group.

![admin-izvestaji-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi/06-admin-izvestaji-otvara-meni.png)

## 7. Pregled

We choose “Pregled”.

![admin-izvestaji-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi/07-admin-izvestaji-bira-stavku.png)

## 8. Business reports

Reports show appointments, the shop, packages, commissions and coupons: the current period live, earlier ones saved.

![izvestaji-pregled](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi/08-izvestaji-pregled.png)

## 9. “Sistem” menu

We open the “Sistem” group.

![admin-logovi-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi/09-admin-logovi-otvara-meni.png)

## 10. Logovi

We choose “Logovi”.

![admin-logovi-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi/10-admin-logovi-bira-stavku.png)

## 11. Logs

The logs show who did what in the system (audit trail) and the state of the site, the first place to look when something is unclear.

![logovi-pregled](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi/11-logovi-pregled.png)
