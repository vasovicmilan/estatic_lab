# Booking an appointment

The full booking flow: a customer picks a service and time slot and confirms the booking, then an admin confirms and completes the appointment.

_titl je zapečen u video - ne treba spoljni .vtt fajl_

## 1. Register

The customer registers by filling in a form with their name, email, and password.

![registracija](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/01-registracija.png)

## 2. Log in

After registering, the customer logs into their new account.

![prijava](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/02-prijava.png)

## 3. Open the service page

The customer opens the page for the chosen service, where they see the description and available variants (e.g. duration and price).

![otvaranje-stranice-usluge](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/03-otvaranje-stranice-usluge.png)

## 4. Choose a service variant

Clicking "Select" chooses the specific service variant and moves on to picking a time slot.

![izbor-varijante-usluge](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/04-izbor-varijante-usluge.png)

## 5. Pick a date and time slot

The customer picks a date; the system shows the free slots for that day based on employee working hours, and the customer clicks the desired slot.

![izbor-datuma-i-termina](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/05-izbor-datuma-i-termina.png)

## 6. Enter contact details

The customer enters a contact phone number before confirming the booking.

![unos-kontakt-podataka](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/06-unos-kontakt-podataka.png)

## 7. Confirm the booking

The customer confirms the booking. The appointment is created with status "pending" until an admin confirms it.

![potvrda-zakazivanja](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/07-potvrda-zakazivanja.png)

## 8. Admin logs in

The admin logs into their account to process the new appointment.

![admin-prijava](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/08-admin-prijava.png)

## 9. Open the account menu

The admin clicks the dropdown showing their name in the top-right corner.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/09-admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni.png)

## 10. Enter the admin panel

The admin picks "Admin panel" from the menu, opening the site's admin area.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/10-admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel.png)

## 11. Open the "Scheduling" menu

The admin clicks the "Scheduling" group in the admin menu, seeing the available items.

![admin-termini-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/11-admin-termini-otvara-meni.png)

## 12. Open the appointments list

The admin picks "Appointments" from the menu, opening the list of all appointments.

![admin-termini-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/12-admin-termini-bira-stavku.png)

## 13. Search for the appointment

The admin searches the list by the customer's email to quickly find the new appointment.

![admin-termini-pretraga](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/13-admin-termini-pretraga.png)

## 14. Open the appointment from the results

The admin opens the appointment's details by clicking the icon in the table row.

![admin-termini-otvaranje-rezultata](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/14-admin-termini-otvaranje-rezultata.png)

## 15. Admin confirms the appointment

The admin confirms the appointment. Only now does the customer have a firm, confirmed slot on the calendar.

![admin-potvrdjuje-termin](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/15-admin-potvrdjuje-termin.png)

## 16. Admin completes the appointment

Once the service has actually been delivered, the admin marks the appointment as completed. This is also the point where employee/partner commission, if applicable, gets recorded.

![admin-zavrsava-termin](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/16-admin-zavrsava-termin.png)
