# Booking an appointment

The full booking flow: the customer registers, picks a service, date and time via the “Zakažite termin” button and confirms, then an admin confirms and completes the appointment. Every click is shown, on both sides.

<video src="../../videos/zakazivanje-termina/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

We start on the site's home page.

![otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/01-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Registration

We click “Registracija” and the account form opens.

![registracija-klik-registracija](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/02-registracija-klik-registracija.png)

## 3. Fill in the form

We type the first name, last name, email and password (twice) and submit the form. The account is created.

![registracija](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/03-registracija.png)

## 4. Log in

After registering we are already on the login page. We type the email and password and log in.

![prijava](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/04-prijava.png)

## 5. Book an appointment

We click the “Zakažite termin” button - it leads straight to the list of services.

![nav-zakazite-termin](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/05-nav-zakazite-termin.png)

## 6. Choosing a service

In the list of services we click the treatment we want. Its page shows the description and the variants (duration and price).

![otvaranje-stranice-usluge](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/06-otvaranje-stranice-usluge.png)

## 7. Choosing a variant

We click “Zakaži” on the variant we want and move on to choosing a time.

![izbor-varijante-usluge](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/07-izbor-varijante-usluge.png)

## 8. Choosing a date

We choose a date - the field is highlighted. The system shows the free slots for that day, based on the employees' working hours.

![izbor-datuma](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/08-izbor-datuma.png)

## 9. Choosing a time slot

We click the time we want.

![izbor-termina](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/09-izbor-termina.png)

## 10. Enter contact details

We type a contact phone number before confirming the booking.

![unos-kontakt-podataka](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/10-unos-kontakt-podataka.png)

## 11. Confirm the booking

We click “Potvrdi zakazivanje”. The appointment is created as “pending” until an admin confirms it.

![potvrda-zakazivanja](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/11-potvrda-zakazivanja.png)

## 12. Home page

The administrator starts on the site's home page.

![admin-otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/12-admin-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 13. Log in

We click “Prijava”.

![admin-login-klik-prijava](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/13-admin-login-klik-prijava.png)

## 14. Admin logs in

The administrator types the email and password and logs in.

![admin-prijava](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/14-admin-prijava.png)

## 15. Open the account menu

The admin clicks the dropdown with their name in the top right corner.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/15-admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni.png)

## 16. Enter the admin panel

The admin chooses “Admin panel” from the menu, which opens the admin part of the site.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/16-admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel.png)

## 17. Open the “Zakazivanje” menu

The admin clicks the “Zakazivanje” group in the admin menu to see its items.

![admin-termini-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/17-admin-termini-otvara-meni.png)

## 18. Open the appointment list

The admin chooses “Termini” - the list of all appointments opens.

![admin-termini-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/18-admin-termini-bira-stavku.png)

## 19. Search for the appointment

The admin searches the list by the customer's email to find the new appointment quickly.

![admin-termini-pretraga](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/19-admin-termini-pretraga.png)

## 20. Open the appointment from the results

The admin opens the appointment's details with the icon in the table row.

![admin-termini-otvaranje-rezultata](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/20-admin-termini-otvaranje-rezultata.png)

## 21. Admin confirms the appointment

The admin confirms the appointment. Only now does the customer have a firm, confirmed slot in the calendar.

![admin-potvrdjuje-termin](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/21-admin-potvrdjuje-termin.png)

## 22. Admin completes the appointment

Once the service has really been provided, the admin marks the appointment as completed. That is also when a partner's/employee's commission, if any, is calculated.

![admin-zavrsava-termin](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina/22-admin-zavrsava-termin.png)
