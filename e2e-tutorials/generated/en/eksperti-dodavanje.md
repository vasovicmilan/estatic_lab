# Experts - adding a team member

An administrator adds a team member in “Eksperti”, who appears on the public “Naš tim” page at once.

<video src="../../videos/eksperti-dodavanje/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

The administrator starts on the site's home page.

![admin-otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje/01-admin-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Log in

We click “Prijava”.

![admin-login-klik-prijava](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje/02-admin-login-klik-prijava.png)

## 3. Admin logs in

The administrator types the email and password and logs in.

![admin-prijava](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje/03-admin-prijava.png)

## 4. Open the account menu

The admin clicks the dropdown with their name in the top right corner.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje/04-admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni.png)

## 5. Enter the admin panel

The admin chooses “Admin panel” from the menu, which opens the admin part of the site.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje/05-admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel.png)

## 6. “Tim” menu

We open the “Tim” group.

![admin-eksperti-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje/06-admin-eksperti-otvara-meni.png)

## 7. Eksperti

We choose “Eksperti”.

![admin-eksperti-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje/07-admin-eksperti-bira-stavku.png)

## 8. New expert

We click “Novi ekspert”. An expert is a team member shown on the public “Naš tim” page; it is not the same as an employee account.

![admin-eksperti-klik-na-dodaj](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje/08-admin-eksperti-klik-na-dodaj.png)

## 9. First and last name

We type the first and last name.

![ekspert-ime](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje/09-ekspert-ime.png)

## 10. Title and bio

The title and short bio are shown on the card.

![ekspert-titula](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje/10-ekspert-titula.png)

## 11. Photo

The photo is required, with alt text.

![ekspert-slika](../../screenshots/eksperti-dodavanje/11-ekspert-slika.png)
