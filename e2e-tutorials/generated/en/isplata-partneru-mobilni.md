# Partner payout (phone)

A partner requests a payout of earned commission from their account, an admin approves it and marks it paid, and the partner sees in their history that it's done. Every menu click is shown, on a phone, for both the partner and the admin.

<video src="../../videos/isplata-partneru-mobilni/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

We start on the site's home page.

![otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/isplata-partneru-mobilni/01-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button in the top right corner. We tap it.

![login-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/isplata-partneru-mobilni/02-login-otvara-meni.png)

## 3. Log in

In the menu we tap “Prijava”.

![login-klik-prijava](../../screenshots/isplata-partneru-mobilni/03-login-klik-prijava.png)

## 4. Enter your login details

We enter the email and password and log in.

![unos-prijave](../../screenshots/isplata-partneru-mobilni/04-unos-prijave.png)

## 5. Account menu

After logging in we open the menu again. At the bottom is a section with our name and account links - the page scrolls to it.

![portal-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/isplata-partneru-mobilni/05-portal-otvara-meni.png)

## 6. Partner account

We tap “Partnerski nalog”.

![portal-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/isplata-partneru-mobilni/06-portal-bira-stavku.png)

## 7. Available for payout

The partner account shows how much commission is earned and how much is available for payout.

![pregled-stanja](../../screenshots/isplata-partneru-mobilni/07-pregled-stanja.png)

## 8. Request a payout

The partner enters an amount and submits a request. They can't request more than what's available.

![zahtev-za-isplatu](../../screenshots/isplata-partneru-mobilni/08-zahtev-za-isplatu.png)

## 9. Reserved and remaining

The requested amount moves to “Rezervisano” and the form only allows what's left - the same amount can't be requested twice.

![rezervisano-i-preostalo](../../screenshots/isplata-partneru-mobilni/09-rezervisano-i-preostalo.png)

## 10. Payouts tab

Tapping “Isplate” in the bottom bar switches to that tab. It lists all payout requests.

![tab-isplate](../../screenshots/isplata-partneru-mobilni/10-tab-isplate.png)

## 11. Request pending

The request has status “Zatraženo” until the admin processes it.

![zahtev-na-cekanju](../../screenshots/isplata-partneru-mobilni/11-zahtev-na-cekanju.png)

## 12. Admin logs in

The administrator logs in.

![admin-prijava](../../screenshots/isplata-partneru-mobilni/12-admin-prijava.png)

## 13. Menu

On a phone the admin opens the main menu with the three-line button.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni](../../screenshots/isplata-partneru-mobilni/13-admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni.png)

## 14. Admin panel

In the “Admin” section they tap “Admin panel”.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel](../../screenshots/isplata-partneru-mobilni/14-admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel.png)

## 15. Admin menu

The admin panel has its own menu behind a three-line button. We open it and choose the “Partnerski program” group.

![admin-isplate-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/isplata-partneru-mobilni/15-admin-isplate-otvara-meni.png)

## 16. Payouts

We tap “Isplate” - the list holds requests from both employees and partners.

![admin-isplate-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/isplata-partneru-mobilni/16-admin-isplate-bira-stavku.png)

## 17. Request details

The details of the partner's request open, with the amount and status.

![admin-otvara-zahtev](../../screenshots/isplata-partneru-mobilni/17-admin-otvara-zahtev.png)

## 18. Approve

The admin approves the request and confirms in the dialog.

![admin-odobrava](../../screenshots/isplata-partneru-mobilni/18-admin-odobrava.png)

## 19. Mark as paid

Once the money has actually been paid out, the request is marked as paid.

![admin-oznacava-isplaceno](../../screenshots/isplata-partneru-mobilni/19-admin-oznacava-isplaceno.png)

## 20. Payout history

The partner opens “Isplate” again and sees the status “Isplaćeno”.

![istorija-isplata](../../screenshots/isplata-partneru-mobilni/20-istorija-isplata.png)

## 21. Balance after payout

On the overview, the paid amount now counts as paid out.

![stanje-posle-isplate](../../screenshots/isplata-partneru-mobilni/21-stanje-posle-isplate.png)
