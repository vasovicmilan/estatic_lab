# Newsletter campaign - preparation

An administrator prepares a campaign: internal name, subject, segment and block content, then saves a draft.

<video src="../../videos/kampanja-newsletter/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

The administrator starts on the site's home page.

![admin-otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/kampanja-newsletter/01-admin-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Log in

We click “Prijava”.

![admin-login-klik-prijava](../../screenshots/kampanja-newsletter/02-admin-login-klik-prijava.png)

## 3. Admin logs in

The administrator types the email and password and logs in.

![admin-prijava](../../screenshots/kampanja-newsletter/03-admin-prijava.png)

## 4. Open the account menu

The admin clicks the dropdown with their name in the top right corner.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni](../../screenshots/kampanja-newsletter/04-admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni.png)

## 5. Enter the admin panel

The admin chooses “Admin panel” from the menu, which opens the admin part of the site.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel](../../screenshots/kampanja-newsletter/05-admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel.png)

## 6. “Sadržaj i marketing” menu

We open the “Sadržaj i marketing” group.

![admin-kampanje-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/kampanja-newsletter/06-admin-kampanje-otvara-meni.png)

## 7. Kampanje

We choose “Kampanje”.

![admin-kampanje-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/kampanja-newsletter/07-admin-kampanje-bira-stavku.png)

## 8. New campaign

We click “Nova kampanja”. A campaign is an email we send to newsletter subscribers.

![admin-kampanje-klik-na-dodaj](../../screenshots/kampanja-newsletter/08-admin-kampanje-klik-na-dodaj.png)

## 9. Internal name

The internal name is only visible to us in the panel; subscribers never receive it.

![kampanja-naziv](../../screenshots/kampanja-newsletter/09-kampanja-naziv.png)

## 10. Email subject

The subject is what the subscriber sees in their inbox, so it should be clear and short.

![kampanja-predmet](../../screenshots/kampanja-newsletter/10-kampanja-predmet.png)

## 11. Segment

We choose who receives it, by interests. If nothing is selected, all active subscribers receive it.

![kampanja-segment](../../screenshots/kampanja-newsletter/11-kampanja-segment.png)

## 12. Content

The email is built from blocks: heading, paragraph, image, button, service or product. We add a paragraph.

![kampanja-sadrzaj](../../screenshots/kampanja-newsletter/12-kampanja-sadrzaj.png)

## 13. Save as draft

We click “Sačuvaj kao nacrt”. Nothing is sent until we schedule the campaign or send it by hand.

![kampanja-cuvanje](../../screenshots/kampanja-newsletter/13-kampanja-cuvanje.png)
