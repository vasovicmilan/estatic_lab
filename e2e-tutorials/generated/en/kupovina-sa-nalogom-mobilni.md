# Buying with an account (phone)

A registered customer: registration also collects a phone number, an address is saved once on the “Moje adrese” page, and at checkout the customer either reuses it or types a new one. Every page is reached by clicking through the menu. Recorded on a phone (390x844).

## 1. Home page

We start on the site's home page.

![otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/01-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button. We tap it to reach the “Registracija” button.

![registracija-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/02-registracija-otvara-meni.png)

## 3. Registration

We tap “Registracija” and the account form opens.

![registracija-klik-registracija](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/03-registracija-klik-registracija.png)

## 4. Registration with a phone number

Besides the name, email and password we also enter a phone number - the field is optional but makes later checkout easier.

![registracija-sa-telefonom](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/04-registracija-sa-telefonom.png)

## 5. Log in

After registering we are already on the login page. We type the email and password and log in.

![prijava](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/05-prijava.png)

## 6. Account menu

We tap our name (on a phone: open the menu and scroll to the section with our name).

![nalog-adrese-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/06-nalog-adrese-otvara-meni.png)

## 7. My addresses

We choose “Moje adrese” - this is where delivery addresses are saved.

![nalog-adrese-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/07-nalog-adrese-bira-stavku.png)

## 8. Save an address

We type the street, number, city and postal code and tap “Sačuvaj adresu”. An address is only ever saved here - checkout never writes one by itself.

![cuvanje-adrese](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/08-cuvanje-adrese.png)

## 9. Account menu

Account pages have their own slim header. We tap our name in the top right corner to open the menu.

![portal-nazad-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/09-portal-nazad-otvara-meni.png)

## 10. Back to the site

We choose “Nazad na sajt” and return to the home page.

![portal-nazad-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/10-portal-nazad-bira-stavku.png)

## 11. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button in the top right corner. We tap it.

![nav-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/11-nav-otvara-meni.png)

## 12. Shop

In the menu we tap “Prodavnica” - the products that can be ordered.

![nav-prodavnica](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/12-nav-prodavnica.png)

## 13. Choosing the first product

In the product list we tap the first product.

![otvaranje-prvog-proizvoda](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/13-otvaranje-prvog-proizvoda.png)

## 14. Add to cart

We tap “Dodaj u korpu” - the system opens the cart straight away.

![dodavanje-u-korpu-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/14-dodavanje-u-korpu-1.png)

## 15. Go to checkout

In the cart we tap “Nastavi na naplatu”. The email and phone are already filled in because we are logged in.

![prelazak-na-naplatu-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/15-prelazak-na-naplatu-1.png)

## 16. Pick the saved address

At checkout we pick the address we saved in the “Sačuvana adresa” dropdown - the fields fill in by themselves.

![izbor-sacuvane-adrese](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/16-izbor-sacuvane-adrese.png)

## 17. Confirm the order

We tap “Potvrdi porudžbinu”.

![potvrda-porudzbine-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/17-potvrda-porudzbine-1.png)

## 18. Confirm via the link

The link from the confirmation email finalizes the order (simulated here by opening the link directly).

![email-potvrde-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/18-email-potvrde-1.png)

## 19. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button in the top right corner. We tap it.

![nav-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/19-nav-otvara-meni.png)

## 20. Shop

In the menu we tap “Prodavnica” - the products that can be ordered.

![nav-prodavnica](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/20-nav-prodavnica.png)

## 21. Choosing the second product

For the second purchase we tap “Prodavnica” again and pick the second product from the list.

![otvaranje-drugog-proizvoda](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/21-otvaranje-drugog-proizvoda.png)

## 22. Add to cart

We tap “Dodaj u korpu” - the system opens the cart.

![dodavanje-u-korpu-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/22-dodavanje-u-korpu-2.png)

## 23. Go to checkout

In the cart we tap “Nastavi na naplatu”.

![prelazak-na-naplatu-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/23-prelazak-na-naplatu-2.png)

## 24. Enter a new address

This time we keep “Unesite novu adresu” and type a different address.

![unos-nove-adrese](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/24-unos-nove-adrese.png)

## 25. Apply the coupon

We type the coupon code and tap the apply button - the reduced amount shows immediately.

![primena-kupona-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/25-primena-kupona-2.png)

## 26. Confirm the order

We tap “Potvrdi porudžbinu”.

![potvrda-porudzbine-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/26-potvrda-porudzbine-2.png)

## 27. Confirm via the link

The email link finalizes the second order as well.

![email-potvrde-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom-mobilni/27-email-potvrde-2.png)
