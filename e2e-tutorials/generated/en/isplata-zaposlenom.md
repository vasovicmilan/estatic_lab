# Employee payout

An employee requests a payout of their earned commission, an admin approves it and marks it paid. The same mechanism works identically for partners, through their own separate account.

## 1. Employee logs in

The employee logs into their account.

![prijava-zaposlenog](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom/01-prijava-zaposlenog.png)

## 2. View earned balance

On their account, the employee sees how much they've earned so far.

![pregled-stanja](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom/02-pregled-stanja.png)

## 3. Request a payout

The employee enters the desired amount and submits a payout request.

![zahtev-za-isplatu](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom/03-zahtev-za-isplatu.png)

## 4. Remaining balance

After the request, the form itself shows how much is left for a further request - an already-reserved amount can't be requested twice.

![preostalo-stanje](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom/04-preostalo-stanje.png)

## 5. Admin logs in

The admin logs in to process the payout request.

![admin-prijava](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom/05-admin-prijava.png)

## 6. Open the account menu

The admin clicks the dropdown showing their name in the top-right corner.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom/06-admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni.png)

## 7. Enter the admin panel

The admin picks "Admin panel" from the menu, opening the site's admin area.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom/07-admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel.png)

## 8. Open the "Partner program" menu

The admin clicks the "Partner program" group in the admin menu.

![admin-isplate-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom/08-admin-isplate-otvara-meni.png)

## 9. Open payouts

The admin picks "Payouts" from the menu.

![admin-isplate-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom/09-admin-isplate-bira-stavku.png)

## 10. Open the request

The admin opens the payout request's details.

![admin-otvara-zahtev](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom/10-admin-otvara-zahtev.png)

## 11. Approve the request

The admin approves the request.

![admin-odobrava](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom/11-admin-odobrava.png)

## 12. Mark as paid

Once the money has actually been transferred (outside the system, e.g. via bank transfer), the admin marks the request as paid.

![admin-oznacava-isplaceno](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom/12-admin-oznacava-isplaceno.png)

## 13. Payout history

The employee immediately sees in their account that the payout was completed.

![istorija-isplata](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom/13-istorija-isplata.png)
