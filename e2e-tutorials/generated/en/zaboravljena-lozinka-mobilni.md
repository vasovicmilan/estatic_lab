# Forgotten password (phone)

A customer who forgot their password requests a link, sets a new one and logs in. Recorded on a phone (390x844).

<video src="../../videos/zaboravljena-lozinka-mobilni/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

We start on the site's home page.

![otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/zaboravljena-lozinka-mobilni/01-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button in the top right corner. We tap it.

![login-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/zaboravljena-lozinka-mobilni/02-login-otvara-meni.png)

## 3. Log in

We tap “Prijava” at the top of the page.

![login-klik-prijava](../../screenshots/zaboravljena-lozinka-mobilni/03-login-klik-prijava.png)

## 4. Forgot your password?

On the login page we tap “Zaboravili ste lozinku?”.

![zaboravljena-link](../../screenshots/zaboravljena-lozinka-mobilni/04-zaboravljena-link.png)

## 5. Email address

We type the account's email address.

![zaboravljena-email](../../screenshots/zaboravljena-lozinka-mobilni/05-zaboravljena-email.png)

## 6. Send the link

We tap “Pošalji link”. A link to set a new password is emailed, and the message is the same whether or not the account exists.

![zaboravljena-slanje](../../screenshots/zaboravljena-lozinka-mobilni/06-zaboravljena-slanje.png)

## 7. The link from the email

We open the link from the email. It is valid for a limited time.

![link-iz-mejla](../../screenshots/zaboravljena-lozinka-mobilni/07-link-iz-mejla.png)

## 8. New password

We type the new password and repeat it (at least 8 characters).

![nova-lozinka](../../screenshots/zaboravljena-lozinka-mobilni/08-nova-lozinka.png)

## 9. Save the password

We tap “Sačuvaj lozinku”. A confirmation appears and we are taken back to the login.

![nova-lozinka-cuvanje](../../screenshots/zaboravljena-lozinka-mobilni/09-nova-lozinka-cuvanje.png)

## 10. Log in

We log in with the new password.

![prijava-novom-lozinkom](../../screenshots/zaboravljena-lozinka-mobilni/10-prijava-novom-lozinkom.png)
