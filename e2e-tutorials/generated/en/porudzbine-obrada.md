# Handling an order from receipt to delivery

An administrator takes an order through the statuses: processing, shipped, delivered, completed.

<video src="../../videos/porudzbine-obrada/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

The administrator starts on the site's home page.

![admin-otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/porudzbine-obrada/01-admin-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Log in

We click “Prijava”.

![admin-login-klik-prijava](../../screenshots/porudzbine-obrada/02-admin-login-klik-prijava.png)

## 3. Admin logs in

The administrator types the email and password and logs in.

![admin-prijava](../../screenshots/porudzbine-obrada/03-admin-prijava.png)

## 4. Open the account menu

The admin clicks the dropdown with their name in the top right corner.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni](../../screenshots/porudzbine-obrada/04-admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni.png)

## 5. Enter the admin panel

The admin chooses “Admin panel” from the menu, which opens the admin part of the site.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel](../../screenshots/porudzbine-obrada/05-admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel.png)

## 6. “Prodavnica” menu

We open the “Prodavnica” group.

![admin-porudzbine-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/porudzbine-obrada/06-admin-porudzbine-otvara-meni.png)

## 7. Porudžbine

We choose “Porudžbine”.

![admin-porudzbine-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/porudzbine-obrada/07-admin-porudzbine-bira-stavku.png)

## 8. Search orders

We look the order up by name, email or product title.

![admin-porudzbine-pretraga](../../screenshots/porudzbine-obrada/08-admin-porudzbine-pretraga.png)

## 9. Order details

We open the order details.

![admin-porudzbine-otvaranje-rezultata](../../screenshots/porudzbine-obrada/09-admin-porudzbine-otvaranje-rezultata.png)

## 10. A new order

A new order is “pending”. The buttons below lead it through the whole journey to the customer, step by step.

![porudzbina-pregled](../../screenshots/porudzbine-obrada/10-porudzbina-pregled.png)

## 11. Processing

We click “Označi kao u obradi” when we start preparing the parcel.

![porudzbina-obrada](../../screenshots/porudzbine-obrada/11-porudzbina-obrada.png)

## 12. Shipped

When the parcel is handed to the courier, we click “Označi kao poslato”.

![porudzbina-slanje](../../screenshots/porudzbine-obrada/12-porudzbina-slanje.png)

## 13. Delivered

Once the courier has delivered it, we click “Označi kao dostavljeno”.

![porudzbina-dostava](../../screenshots/porudzbine-obrada/13-porudzbina-dostava.png)

## 14. Completed

Finally we click “Označi kao završeno”. That is when the partner's commission is calculated, if the order came through a coupon.

![porudzbina-zavrsetak](../../screenshots/porudzbine-obrada/14-porudzbina-zavrsetak.png)
