# My account - overview and logging out

A customer's own account from registration to logging out: the account menu, “Moj profil”, all six account tabs (top pills on a computer, the bottom bar on a phone), the way back to the site and “Odjava”. No address is ever typed.

<video src="../../videos/moj-nalog-pregled/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

We start on the site's home page.

![otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/moj-nalog-pregled/01-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Registration

We click “Registracija” and the account form opens.

![registracija-klik-registracija](../../screenshots/moj-nalog-pregled/02-registracija-klik-registracija.png)

## 3. Fill in the form

We type the first name, last name, email and password (twice) and submit the form. The account is created.

![registracija](../../screenshots/moj-nalog-pregled/03-registracija.png)

## 4. Log in

After registering we are already on the login page. We type the email and password and log in.

![prijava](../../screenshots/moj-nalog-pregled/04-prijava.png)

## 5. Account menu

After logging in our name appears - we click it (on a phone: open the menu and scroll to the section with our name).

![nalog-profil-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/moj-nalog-pregled/05-nalog-profil-otvara-meni.png)

## 6. My profile

We choose “Moj profil”.

![nalog-profil-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/moj-nalog-pregled/06-nalog-profil-bira-stavku.png)

## 7. My account

“Moj nalog” has opened - the personal details and tabs for everything about our account.

![pregled-profila](../../screenshots/moj-nalog-pregled/07-pregled-profila.png)

## 8. Appointments tab

The “Termini” tab holds all our booked and past appointments.

![tab-termini](../../screenshots/moj-nalog-pregled/08-tab-termini.png)

## 9. Orders tab

“Porudžbine” - the purchase history and the status of each order.

![tab-porudzbine](../../screenshots/moj-nalog-pregled/09-tab-porudzbine.png)

## 10. Addresses tab

“Adrese” - saved delivery addresses, and a form for a new one.

![tab-adrese](../../screenshots/moj-nalog-pregled/10-tab-adrese.png)

## 11. Activity tab

“Aktivnost” - everything that happened on our account, for example logins.

![tab-aktivnost](../../screenshots/moj-nalog-pregled/11-tab-aktivnost.png)

## 12. Settings tab

“Podešavanja” - personal details, changing the password and deactivating the account.

![tab-podesavanja](../../screenshots/moj-nalog-pregled/12-tab-podesavanja.png)

## 13. Back to Profile

We click “Profil” and are back at the start of the account.

![tab-profil](../../screenshots/moj-nalog-pregled/13-tab-profil.png)

## 14. Account menu

Account pages have their own slim header. We click our name in the top right corner to open the menu.

![portal-nazad-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/moj-nalog-pregled/14-portal-nazad-otvara-meni.png)

## 15. Back to the site

We choose “Nazad na sajt” and return to the home page.

![portal-nazad-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/moj-nalog-pregled/15-portal-nazad-bira-stavku.png)

## 16. Account menu

We open the account menu again (on a phone: the menu, then scroll to the section with our name).

![odjava-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/moj-nalog-pregled/16-odjava-otvara-meni.png)

## 17. Log out

We choose “Odjava” - the session ends.

![odjava-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/moj-nalog-pregled/17-odjava-bira-stavku.png)

## 18. Logged out

The session has ended - the header offers “Prijava” and “Registracija” again.

![odjavljen](../../screenshots/moj-nalog-pregled/18-odjavljen.png)
