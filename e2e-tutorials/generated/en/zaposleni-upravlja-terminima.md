# Employee managing their own appointments

An employee has their own dedicated account area for their appointments - confirming, completing, and reviewing earned commission - entirely separate from the admin panel.

## 1. Employee logs in

The employee logs into their account.

![prijava-zaposlenog](../../screenshots/zaposleni-upravlja-terminima/01-prijava-zaposlenog.png)

## 2. View my appointments

In the "My appointments" section, the employee sees only appointments assigned to them.

![pregled-mojih-termina](../../screenshots/zaposleni-upravlja-terminima/02-pregled-mojih-termina.png)

## 3. Appointment details

Clicking the appointment opens its details, with options to confirm and complete it.

![otvaranje-detalja-termina](../../screenshots/zaposleni-upravlja-terminima/03-otvaranje-detalja-termina.png)

## 4. Confirm the appointment

The employee confirms the appointment - the customer now has a firm, confirmed slot.

![potvrda-termina](../../screenshots/zaposleni-upravlja-terminima/04-potvrda-termina.png)

## 5. Complete the appointment

Once the service has been delivered, the employee marks it completed - this is also when their commission gets recorded.

![zavrsavanje-termina](../../screenshots/zaposleni-upravlja-terminima/05-zavrsavanje-termina.png)

## 6. View commission

In the "Commissions" section, the employee immediately sees the earned percentage for this appointment.

![pregled-provizije](../../screenshots/zaposleni-upravlja-terminima/06-pregled-provizije.png)
