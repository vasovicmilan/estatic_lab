# Buying without an account (guest) (phone)

A customer buys with no registration at all: picks a product in the shop, enters their details at checkout and the system creates an account in the background. Two purchases are shown (the second with a coupon) - each with a new guest, so the automatic account creation happens both times. Recorded on a phone (390x844).

## 1. Home page

We start on the site's home page.

![otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga-mobilni/01-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button in the top right corner. We tap it.

![nav-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga-mobilni/02-nav-otvara-meni.png)

## 3. Shop

In the menu we tap “Prodavnica” - the products that can be ordered.

![nav-prodavnica](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga-mobilni/03-nav-prodavnica.png)

## 4. Choosing the first product

In the product list we tap the first product.

![otvaranje-prvog-proizvoda](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga-mobilni/04-otvaranje-prvog-proizvoda.png)

## 5. Add to cart

We tap “Dodaj u korpu” - the system opens the cart straight away.

![dodavanje-u-korpu-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga-mobilni/05-dodavanje-u-korpu-1.png)

## 6. Go to checkout

In the cart we tap “Nastavi na naplatu”. We are not logged in - the system points that out and explains that an account will be created automatically.

![prelazak-na-naplatu-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga-mobilni/06-prelazak-na-naplatu-1.png)

## 7. Guest details

Nothing is pre-filled - we type the first name, last name, email, phone and delivery address.

![unos-podataka-kao-gost-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga-mobilni/07-unos-podataka-kao-gost-1.png)

## 8. Confirm the order

We tap “Potvrdi porudžbinu”.

![potvrda-porudzbine-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga-mobilni/08-potvrda-porudzbine-1.png)

## 9. Account created automatically

In the background the system has created a real user account for this email - the customer did not have to ask for it.

![nalog-automatski-kreiran-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga-mobilni/09-nalog-automatski-kreiran-1.png)

## 10. Confirm via the link

The link from the confirmation email finalizes the order (simulated here by opening the link directly).

![email-potvrde-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga-mobilni/10-email-potvrde-1.png)

## 11. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button in the top right corner. We tap it.

![nav-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga-mobilni/11-nav-otvara-meni.png)

## 12. Shop

In the menu we tap “Prodavnica” - the products that can be ordered.

![nav-prodavnica](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga-mobilni/12-nav-prodavnica.png)

## 13. Choosing the second product

For the second purchase we tap “Prodavnica” again and pick the second product from the list.

![otvaranje-drugog-proizvoda](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga-mobilni/13-otvaranje-drugog-proizvoda.png)

We tap “Dodaj u korpu” - the system opens the cart.

![dodavanje-u-korpu-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga-mobilni/14-dodavanje-u-korpu-2.png)

## 15. Go to checkout

In the cart we tap “Nastavi na naplatu”.

![prelazak-na-naplatu-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-bez-naloga-mobilni/15-prelazak-na-naplatu-2.png)

A second guest with a different email - again we type all the details from scratch.
