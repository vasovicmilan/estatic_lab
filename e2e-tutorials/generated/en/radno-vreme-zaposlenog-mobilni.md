# Employee working hours (phone)

How an employee sets their own working hours, and how that immediately affects which appointment slots are available for booking.

<video src="../../videos/radno-vreme-zaposlenog-mobilni/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. No working hours, no slots

While the employee has no working hours set for a given day, the customer sees no available slots on the booking page.

![bez-radnog-vremena-nema-termina](../../screenshots/radno-vreme-zaposlenog-mobilni/01-bez-radnog-vremena-nema-termina.png)

## 2. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button in the top right corner. We tap it.

![login-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/radno-vreme-zaposlenog-mobilni/02-login-otvara-meni.png)

## 3. Log in

In the menu we tap “Prijava”.

![login-klik-prijava](../../screenshots/radno-vreme-zaposlenog-mobilni/03-login-klik-prijava.png)

## 4. Enter your login details

The employee enters the email and password and logs in.

![unos-prijave](../../screenshots/radno-vreme-zaposlenog-mobilni/04-unos-prijave.png)

## 5. Account menu

After logging in we open the menu again. At the bottom is a section with the employee's name and account links - the page scrolls to it.

![portal-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/radno-vreme-zaposlenog-mobilni/05-portal-otvara-meni.png)

## 6. My calendar

We tap “Moj kalendar” and enter the employee's account.

![portal-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/radno-vreme-zaposlenog-mobilni/06-portal-bira-stavku.png)

## 7. Profile and working hours

On the bottom bar we tap “Profil”, where the working hours per day are set.

![otvaranje-profila](../../screenshots/radno-vreme-zaposlenog-mobilni/07-otvaranje-profila.png)

## 8. Add a working window

The employee adds a time window for the day (e.g. 09:00-17:00) via the interactive schedule widget.

![dodavanje-radnog-vremena](../../screenshots/radno-vreme-zaposlenog-mobilni/08-dodavanje-radnog-vremena.png)

## 9. Save working hours

Clicking "Save working hours" saves the change.

![cuvanje-radnog-vremena](../../screenshots/radno-vreme-zaposlenog-mobilni/09-cuvanje-radnog-vremena.png)

## 10. Slots are now available

The same booking page, with no further change from the customer's side, now shows available slots within the new working hours.

![termini-sada-dostupni](../../screenshots/radno-vreme-zaposlenog-mobilni/10-termini-sada-dostupni.png)
