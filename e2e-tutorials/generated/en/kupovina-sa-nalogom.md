# Checkout with an account

A registered customer enters their phone at registration, saves an address to their account, then at checkout either reuses it or enters a different one.

<video src="../../videos/kupovina-sa-nalogom/merged.en.burned.webm" controls width="720"></video>

_titl je zapečen u video - ne treba spoljni .vtt fajl_

## 1. Register with a phone number

The customer registers and, unlike other flows, enters a phone number too - the field exists on the form, it's just not required.

![registracija-sa-telefonom](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/01-registracija-sa-telefonom.png)

## 2. Log in

The customer logs into their account.

![prijava](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/02-prijava.png)

## 3. Open saved addresses

In the account's addresses section, the customer sees a form to add a new address.

![otvaranje-adresa](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/03-otvaranje-adresa.png)

## 4. Save an address

The customer enters and saves an address - this does NOT happen automatically after an order, an address is only saved if the customer adds it here themselves.

![cuvanje-adrese](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/04-cuvanje-adrese.png)

## 5. Open the first product

The customer picks a product and adds it to the cart.

![otvaranje-prvog-proizvoda](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/05-otvaranje-prvog-proizvoda.png)

## 6. Proceed to checkout

Since the customer is logged in, the email is already filled in (and locked from editing).

![prelazak-na-naplatu-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/06-prelazak-na-naplatu-1.png)

## 7. Select the saved address

The dropdown offers the previously saved address - the customer selects it and the fields fill in automatically.

![izbor-sacuvane-adrese](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/07-izbor-sacuvane-adrese.png)

## 8. Confirm the order

The customer confirms the first order, with no coupon.

![potvrda-porudzbine-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/08-potvrda-porudzbine-1.png)

## 9. Confirmation email

The customer finalizes the order by clicking the link from the email.

![email-potvrde-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/09-email-potvrde-1.png)

## 10. Open the second product

The same customer buys something else.

![otvaranje-drugog-proizvoda](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/10-otvaranje-drugog-proizvoda.png)

## 11. Proceed to checkout

The customer reaches checkout again for the second order.

![prelazak-na-naplatu-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/11-prelazak-na-naplatu-2.png)

## 12. Enter a new address

This time the customer picks "Enter a new address" from the dropdown instead of the saved one, and types a different address by hand.

![unos-nove-adrese](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/12-unos-nove-adrese.png)

## 13. Apply the coupon

For the second order, the customer applies a coupon code.

![primena-kupona-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/13-primena-kupona-2.png)

## 14. Confirm the order

The customer confirms the second order, with the discount applied.

![potvrda-porudzbine-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/14-potvrda-porudzbine-2.png)

## 15. Confirmation email

The second order is finalized the same way.

![email-potvrde-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/15-email-potvrde-2.png)
