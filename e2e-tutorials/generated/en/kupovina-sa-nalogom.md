# Buying with an account

A registered customer: registration also collects a phone number, an address is saved once on the “Moje adrese” page, and at checkout the customer either reuses it or types a new one. Every page is reached by clicking through the menu.

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

We start on the site's home page.

![otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/01-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Registration

We click “Registracija” and the account form opens.

![registracija-klik-registracija](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/02-registracija-klik-registracija.png)

## 3. Registration with a phone number

Besides the name, email and password we also enter a phone number - the field is optional but makes later checkout easier.

![registracija-sa-telefonom](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/03-registracija-sa-telefonom.png)

## 4. Log in

After registering we are already on the login page. We type the email and password and log in.

![prijava](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/04-prijava.png)

## 5. Account menu

We click our name (on a phone: open the menu and scroll to the section with our name).

![nalog-adrese-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/05-nalog-adrese-otvara-meni.png)

## 6. My addresses

We choose “Moje adrese” - this is where delivery addresses are saved.

![nalog-adrese-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/06-nalog-adrese-bira-stavku.png)

## 7. Save an address

We type the street, number, city and postal code and click “Sačuvaj adresu”. An address is only ever saved here - checkout never writes one by itself.

![cuvanje-adrese](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/07-cuvanje-adrese.png)

## 8. Account menu

Account pages have their own slim header. We click our name in the top right corner to open the menu.

![portal-nazad-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/08-portal-nazad-otvara-meni.png)

## 9. Back to the site

We choose “Nazad na sajt” and return to the home page.

![portal-nazad-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/09-portal-nazad-bira-stavku.png)

## 10. Shop

In the menu we click “Prodavnica” - the products that can be ordered.

![nav-prodavnica](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/10-nav-prodavnica.png)

## 11. Choosing the first product

In the product list we click the first product.

![otvaranje-prvog-proizvoda](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/11-otvaranje-prvog-proizvoda.png)

## 12. Add to cart

We click “Dodaj u korpu” - the system opens the cart straight away.

![dodavanje-u-korpu-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/12-dodavanje-u-korpu-1.png)

## 13. Go to checkout

In the cart we click “Nastavi na naplatu”. The email and phone are already filled in because we are logged in.

![prelazak-na-naplatu-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/13-prelazak-na-naplatu-1.png)

## 14. Pick the saved address

At checkout we pick the address we saved in the “Sačuvana adresa” dropdown - the fields fill in by themselves.

![izbor-sacuvane-adrese](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/14-izbor-sacuvane-adrese.png)

## 15. Confirm the order

We click “Potvrdi porudžbinu”.

![potvrda-porudzbine-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/15-potvrda-porudzbine-1.png)

## 16. Confirm via the link

The link from the confirmation email finalizes the order (simulated here by opening the link directly).

![email-potvrde-1](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/16-email-potvrde-1.png)

## 17. Shop

In the menu we click “Prodavnica” - the products that can be ordered.

![nav-prodavnica](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/17-nav-prodavnica.png)

## 18. Choosing the second product

For the second purchase we click “Prodavnica” again and pick the second product from the list.

![otvaranje-drugog-proizvoda](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/18-otvaranje-drugog-proizvoda.png)

## 19. Add to cart

We click “Dodaj u korpu” - the system opens the cart.

![dodavanje-u-korpu-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/19-dodavanje-u-korpu-2.png)

## 20. Go to checkout

In the cart we click “Nastavi na naplatu”.

![prelazak-na-naplatu-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/20-prelazak-na-naplatu-2.png)

## 21. Enter a new address

This time we keep “Unesite novu adresu” and type a different address.

![unos-nove-adrese](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/21-unos-nove-adrese.png)

## 22. Apply the coupon

We type the coupon code and click the apply button - the reduced amount shows immediately.

![primena-kupona-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/22-primena-kupona-2.png)

## 23. Confirm the order

We click “Potvrdi porudžbinu”.

![potvrda-porudzbine-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/23-potvrda-porudzbine-2.png)

## 24. Confirm via the link

The email link finalizes the second order as well.

![email-potvrde-2](../../screenshots/kupovina-sa-nalogom/24-email-potvrde-2.png)
