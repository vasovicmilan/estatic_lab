# Booking an appointment (phone)

The full booking flow: the customer registers, picks a service, date and time via the “Zakažite termin” button and confirms, then an admin confirms and completes the appointment. Every click is shown, on both sides. Recorded on a phone (390x844).

<video src="../../videos/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

We start on the site's home page.

![otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/01-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button. We tap it to reach the “Registracija” button.

![registracija-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/02-registracija-otvara-meni.png)

## 3. Registration

We tap “Registracija” and the account form opens.

![registracija-klik-registracija](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/03-registracija-klik-registracija.png)

## 4. Fill in the form

We type the first name, last name, email and password (twice) and submit the form. The account is created.

![registracija](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/04-registracija.png)

## 5. Log in

After registering we are already on the login page. We type the email and password and log in.

![prijava](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/05-prijava.png)

## 6. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button in the top right corner. We tap it.

![nav-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/06-nav-otvara-meni.png)

## 7. Book an appointment

We tap the “Zakažite termin” button - it leads straight to the list of services.

![nav-zakazite-termin](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/07-nav-zakazite-termin.png)

## 8. Choosing a service

In the list of services we tap the treatment we want. Its page shows the description and the variants (duration and price).

![otvaranje-stranice-usluge](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/08-otvaranje-stranice-usluge.png)

## 9. Choosing a variant

We tap “Zakaži” on the variant we want and move on to choosing a time.

![izbor-varijante-usluge](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/09-izbor-varijante-usluge.png)

## 10. Choosing a date

We choose a date - the field is highlighted. The system shows the free slots for that day, based on the employees' working hours.

![izbor-datuma](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/10-izbor-datuma.png)

## 11. Choosing a time slot

We tap the time we want.

![izbor-termina](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/11-izbor-termina.png)

## 12. Enter contact details

We type a contact phone number before confirming the booking.

![unos-kontakt-podataka](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/12-unos-kontakt-podataka.png)

## 13. Confirm the booking

We tap “Potvrdi zakazivanje”. The appointment is created as “pending” until an admin confirms it.

![potvrda-zakazivanja](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/13-potvrda-zakazivanja.png)

## 14. Home page

The administrator starts on the site's home page.

![admin-otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/14-admin-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 15. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button in the top right corner. We tap it.

![admin-login-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/15-admin-login-otvara-meni.png)

## 16. Log in

We tap “Prijava”.

![admin-login-klik-prijava](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/16-admin-login-klik-prijava.png)

## 17. Admin logs in

The administrator types the email and password and logs in.

![admin-prijava](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/17-admin-prijava.png)

## 18. Menu

On a phone the admin opens the main menu with the three-line button.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/18-admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni.png)

## 19. Admin panel

In the “Admin” section they tap “Admin panel”.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/19-admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel.png)

## 20. Open the “Zakazivanje” menu

The admin panel has its own menu behind a three-line button. The admin opens it and chooses the “Zakazivanje” group.

![admin-termini-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/20-admin-termini-otvara-meni.png)

## 21. Open the appointment list

The admin chooses “Termini” - the list of all appointments opens.

![admin-termini-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/21-admin-termini-bira-stavku.png)

## 22. Search for the appointment

The admin searches the list by the customer's email to find the new appointment quickly.

![admin-termini-pretraga](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/22-admin-termini-pretraga.png)

## 23. Open the appointment from the results

On a phone the table scrolls sideways - the admin taps the details icon at the end of the row.

![admin-termini-otvaranje-rezultata](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/23-admin-termini-otvaranje-rezultata.png)

## 24. Admin confirms the appointment

The admin confirms the appointment. Only now does the customer have a firm, confirmed slot in the calendar.

![admin-potvrdjuje-termin](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/24-admin-potvrdjuje-termin.png)

## 25. Admin completes the appointment

Once the service has really been provided, the admin marks the appointment as completed. That is also when a partner's/employee's commission, if any, is calculated.

![admin-zavrsava-termin](../../screenshots/zakazivanje-termina-mobilni/25-admin-zavrsava-termin.png)
