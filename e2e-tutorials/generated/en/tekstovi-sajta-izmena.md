# Site texts - editing the “About” page

An administrator edits the “About” intro text in “Tekstovi sajta” and sees it on the public site at once.

<video src="../../videos/tekstovi-sajta-izmena/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

The administrator starts on the site's home page.

![admin-otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/tekstovi-sajta-izmena/01-admin-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Log in

We click “Prijava”.

![admin-login-klik-prijava](../../screenshots/tekstovi-sajta-izmena/02-admin-login-klik-prijava.png)

## 3. Admin logs in

The administrator types the email and password and logs in.

![admin-prijava](../../screenshots/tekstovi-sajta-izmena/03-admin-prijava.png)

## 4. Open the account menu

The admin clicks the dropdown with their name in the top right corner.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni](../../screenshots/tekstovi-sajta-izmena/04-admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni.png)

## 5. Enter the admin panel

The admin chooses “Admin panel” from the menu, which opens the admin part of the site.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel](../../screenshots/tekstovi-sajta-izmena/05-admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel.png)

## 6. “Sadržaj i marketing” menu

We open the “Sadržaj i marketing” group.

![admin-tekstovi-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/tekstovi-sajta-izmena/06-admin-tekstovi-otvara-meni.png)

## 7. Tekstovi sajta

We choose “Tekstovi sajta”.

![admin-tekstovi-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/tekstovi-sajta-izmena/07-admin-tekstovi-bira-stavku.png)

## 8. Choose the text

Every site text has its own card: About, FAQ, privacy, terms, page intros and SEO titles. We click “Izmeni” on the “O nama” card.

![tekstovi-izbor-teksta](../../screenshots/tekstovi-sajta-izmena/08-tekstovi-izbor-teksta.png)

## 9. Edit the text

We change the intro text. HTML such as a link is allowed.

![tekstovi-izmena](../../screenshots/tekstovi-sajta-izmena/09-tekstovi-izmena.png)

## 10. Save

We save the change.

![tekstovi-cuvanje](../../screenshots/tekstovi-sajta-izmena/10-tekstovi-cuvanje.png)

## 11. The public site

We go back to the public site.

![otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/tekstovi-sajta-izmena/11-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 12. The “Više” menu

Less frequently used pages are grouped under “Više”. We click it to open the list.

![nav-vise-otvara](../../screenshots/tekstovi-sajta-izmena/12-nav-vise-otvara.png)

## 13. About us

From the list we pick “O nama” - the story of the salon.

![nav-o-nama](../../screenshots/tekstovi-sajta-izmena/13-nav-o-nama.png)

## 14. The change is live at once

On the “O nama” page the new text is already there, with no restart or code change.

![tekstovi-javna-stranica](../../screenshots/tekstovi-sajta-izmena/14-tekstovi-javna-stranica.png)
