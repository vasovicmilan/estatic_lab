# Business reports and logs (phone)

An administrator looks at the business reports and the system logs, from the admin menu. Recorded on a phone (390x844).

<video src="../../videos/izvestaji-i-logovi-mobilni/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

The administrator starts on the site's home page.

![admin-otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi-mobilni/01-admin-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button in the top right corner. We tap it.

![admin-login-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi-mobilni/02-admin-login-otvara-meni.png)

## 3. Log in

We tap “Prijava”.

![admin-login-klik-prijava](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi-mobilni/03-admin-login-klik-prijava.png)

## 4. Admin logs in

The administrator types the email and password and logs in.

![admin-prijava](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi-mobilni/04-admin-prijava.png)

## 5. Menu

On a phone the admin opens the main menu with the three-line button.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi-mobilni/05-admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni.png)

## 6. Admin panel

In the “Admin” section they tap “Admin panel”.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi-mobilni/06-admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel.png)

## 7. “Poslovni izveštaji” menu

We open the “Poslovni izveštaji” group.

![admin-izvestaji-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi-mobilni/07-admin-izvestaji-otvara-meni.png)

## 8. Pregled

We choose “Pregled”.

![admin-izvestaji-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi-mobilni/08-admin-izvestaji-bira-stavku.png)

## 9. Business reports

Reports show appointments, the shop, packages, commissions and coupons: the current period live, earlier ones saved.

![izvestaji-pregled](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi-mobilni/09-izvestaji-pregled.png)

## 10. “Sistem” menu

We open the “Sistem” group.

![admin-logovi-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi-mobilni/10-admin-logovi-otvara-meni.png)

## 11. Logovi

We choose “Logovi”.

![admin-logovi-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi-mobilni/11-admin-logovi-bira-stavku.png)

## 12. Logs

The logs show who did what in the system (audit trail) and the state of the site, the first place to look when something is unclear.

![logovi-pregled](../../screenshots/izvestaji-i-logovi-mobilni/12-logovi-pregled.png)
