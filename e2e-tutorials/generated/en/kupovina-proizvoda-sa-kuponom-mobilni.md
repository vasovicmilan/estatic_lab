# Buying a product with a coupon (phone)

The standard purchase flow from the home page: a customer registers, picks a product in the shop, adds it to the cart, applies a coupon discount at checkout and confirms the order. Every click is shown. Recorded on a phone (390x844).

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

We start on the site's home page.

![otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/kupovina-proizvoda-sa-kuponom-mobilni/01-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button. We tap it to reach the “Registracija” button.

![registracija-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/kupovina-proizvoda-sa-kuponom-mobilni/02-registracija-otvara-meni.png)

## 3. Registration

We tap “Registracija” and the account form opens.

![registracija-klik-registracija](../../screenshots/kupovina-proizvoda-sa-kuponom-mobilni/03-registracija-klik-registracija.png)

## 4. Fill in the form

We type the first name, last name, email and password (twice) and submit the form. The account is created.

![registracija](../../screenshots/kupovina-proizvoda-sa-kuponom-mobilni/04-registracija.png)

## 5. Log in

After registering we are already on the login page. We type the email and password and log in.

![prijava](../../screenshots/kupovina-proizvoda-sa-kuponom-mobilni/05-prijava.png)

## 6. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button in the top right corner. We tap it.

![nav-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/kupovina-proizvoda-sa-kuponom-mobilni/06-nav-otvara-meni.png)

## 7. Shop

In the menu we tap “Prodavnica” - the products that can be ordered.

![nav-prodavnica](../../screenshots/kupovina-proizvoda-sa-kuponom-mobilni/07-nav-prodavnica.png)

## 8. Choosing a product

In the product list we tap the product we want. Its page shows the description, price and available variants.

![otvaranje-stranice-proizvoda](../../screenshots/kupovina-proizvoda-sa-kuponom-mobilni/08-otvaranje-stranice-proizvoda.png)

## 9. Add to cart

We tap “Dodaj u korpu”. The system adds the product and opens the cart straight away, showing what we picked.

![dodavanje-u-korpu](../../screenshots/kupovina-proizvoda-sa-kuponom-mobilni/09-dodavanje-u-korpu.png)

## 10. Go to checkout

In the cart we tap “Nastavi na naplatu” and the checkout page opens.

![prelazak-na-naplatu](../../screenshots/kupovina-proizvoda-sa-kuponom-mobilni/10-prelazak-na-naplatu.png)

## 11. Enter delivery details

We enter the phone number and delivery address needed to process the order.

![unos-podataka-za-dostavu](../../screenshots/kupovina-proizvoda-sa-kuponom-mobilni/11-unos-podataka-za-dostavu.png)

## 12. Apply the coupon

We type the coupon code and tap the apply button - the system immediately shows the reduced amount.

![primena-kupona](../../screenshots/kupovina-proizvoda-sa-kuponom-mobilni/12-primena-kupona.png)

## 13. Confirm the order

We tap “Potvrdi porudžbinu”. For standard shipping the price is calculated immediately - there is no waiting for a quote as with large/heavy items.

![potvrda-porudzbine](../../screenshots/kupovina-proizvoda-sa-kuponom-mobilni/13-potvrda-porudzbine.png)

## 14. Confirm via the link

Clicking the link in the confirmation email (simulated here by opening the link directly) finalizes the order.

![potvrda-preko-linka](../../screenshots/kupovina-proizvoda-sa-kuponom-mobilni/14-potvrda-preko-linka.png)
