# Employee payout (phone)

An employee requests a payout of earned commission from their account, an admin approves it and marks it paid, and the employee sees in their history that it's done. Every tap is shown, on a phone, for both the employee and the admin.

<video src="../../videos/isplata-zaposlenom-mobilni/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

We start on the site's home page.

![otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom-mobilni/01-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button in the top right corner. We tap it.

![login-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom-mobilni/02-login-otvara-meni.png)

## 3. Log in

In the menu we tap “Prijava”.

![login-klik-prijava](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom-mobilni/03-login-klik-prijava.png)

## 4. Enter your login details

The employee enters the email and password and logs in.

![unos-prijave](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom-mobilni/04-unos-prijave.png)

## 5. Account menu

After logging in we open the menu again. At the bottom is a section with the employee's name and account links - the page scrolls to it.

![portal-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom-mobilni/05-portal-otvara-meni.png)

## 6. My calendar

We tap “Moj kalendar” and enter the employee's account.

![portal-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom-mobilni/06-portal-bira-stavku.png)

## 7. View earned balance

On their account the employee sees how much they've earned so far. The page scrolls to the balance card.

![pregled-stanja](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom-mobilni/07-pregled-stanja.png)

## 8. Request a payout

The employee enters the desired amount and submits a payout request.

![zahtev-za-isplatu](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom-mobilni/08-zahtev-za-isplatu.png)

## 9. Remaining balance

After the request, the form itself shows how much is left for a further request - an already-reserved amount can't be requested twice.

![preostalo-stanje](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom-mobilni/09-preostalo-stanje.png)

## 10. Payouts tab

On the bottom bar we tap “Isplate” to see the requests.

![tab-isplate](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom-mobilni/10-tab-isplate.png)

## 11. Request pending

The request is in the history with the status “Zatraženo” until an admin handles it.

![zahtev-na-cekanju](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom-mobilni/11-zahtev-na-cekanju.png)

## 12. Admin logs in

The admin logs in to process the payout request.

![admin-prijava](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom-mobilni/12-admin-prijava.png)

## 13. Open the account menu

The admin clicks the dropdown showing their name in the top-right corner.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom-mobilni/13-admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni.png)

## 14. Enter the admin panel

The admin picks "Admin panel" from the menu, opening the site's admin area.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom-mobilni/14-admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel.png)

## 15. Open the "Partner program" menu

The admin clicks the "Partner program" group in the admin menu.

![admin-isplate-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom-mobilni/15-admin-isplate-otvara-meni.png)

## 16. Open payouts

The admin picks "Payouts" from the menu.

![admin-isplate-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom-mobilni/16-admin-isplate-bira-stavku.png)

## 17. Open the request

The admin opens the payout request's details.

![admin-otvara-zahtev](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom-mobilni/17-admin-otvara-zahtev.png)

## 18. Approve the request

The admin approves the request.

![admin-odobrava](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom-mobilni/18-admin-odobrava.png)

## 19. Mark as paid

Once the money has actually been transferred (outside the system, e.g. via bank transfer), the admin marks the request as paid.

![admin-oznacava-isplaceno](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom-mobilni/19-admin-oznacava-isplaceno.png)

## 20. Payout history

On the “Isplate” tab the employee immediately sees that the payout was completed.

![istorija-isplata](../../screenshots/isplata-zaposlenom-mobilni/20-istorija-isplata.png)
