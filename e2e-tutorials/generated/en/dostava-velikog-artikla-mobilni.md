# Shipping a large/heavy item (phone)

For large or heavy items the system can't calculate shipping automatically. The customer immediately gets only an informational email that the order is pending a quote - the real email with the price and a confirm link only arrives once an admin enters the actual shipping cost. Recorded on a phone (390x844).
