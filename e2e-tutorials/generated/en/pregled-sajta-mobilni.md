# Site tour - where everything is (phone)

A tour of the public site for someone seeing it for the first time: every item of the main menu, the “Više” group, one service and one product opened from their lists, and the logo as the way back home. Every click is shown - on a computer and on a phone (where the menu is behind the three-line button). Recorded on a phone (390x844).
