# A customer review - from sending to approval

A visitor leaves a review on the home page, and an administrator checks and approves it in “Testimoniali”; only then does it become public.

<video src="../../videos/testimonial-odobravanje/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

We start on the site's home page.

![otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/testimonial-odobravanje/01-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Leave a review

On the home page (and on service, package and product pages) a visitor can leave a review.

![utisak-forma](../../screenshots/testimonial-odobravanje/02-utisak-forma.png)

## 3. Name

They type the name shown with the review.

![utisak-ime](../../screenshots/testimonial-odobravanje/03-utisak-ime.png)

## 4. Rating

They choose a rating from one to five stars.

![utisak-ocena](../../screenshots/testimonial-odobravanje/04-utisak-ocena.png)

## 5. The review

They write the review, at least 10 characters.

![utisak-poruka](../../screenshots/testimonial-odobravanje/05-utisak-poruka.png)

## 6. Consent

Consent to publish it is required.

![utisak-pristanak](../../screenshots/testimonial-odobravanje/06-utisak-pristanak.png)

## 7. Send the review

We click “Pošalji utisak”. The review does not go public at once: it waits for a check.

![utisak-slanje](../../screenshots/testimonial-odobravanje/07-utisak-slanje.png)

## 8. Home page

The administrator starts on the site's home page.

![admin-otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/testimonial-odobravanje/08-admin-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 9. Log in

We click “Prijava”.

![admin-login-klik-prijava](../../screenshots/testimonial-odobravanje/09-admin-login-klik-prijava.png)

## 10. Admin logs in

The administrator types the email and password and logs in.

![admin-prijava](../../screenshots/testimonial-odobravanje/10-admin-prijava.png)

## 11. Open the account menu

The admin clicks the dropdown with their name in the top right corner.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni](../../screenshots/testimonial-odobravanje/11-admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni.png)

## 12. Enter the admin panel

The admin chooses “Admin panel” from the menu, which opens the admin part of the site.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel](../../screenshots/testimonial-odobravanje/12-admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel.png)

## 13. “Sadržaj i marketing” menu

We open the “Sadržaj i marketing” group.

![admin-testimoniali-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/testimonial-odobravanje/13-admin-testimoniali-otvara-meni.png)

## 14. Testimoniali

We choose “Testimoniali”.

![admin-testimoniali-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/testimonial-odobravanje/14-admin-testimoniali-bira-stavku.png)

## 15. Pending review

In the list we find the pending review and open it.

![admin-testimoniali-otvaranje](../../screenshots/testimonial-odobravanje/15-admin-testimoniali-otvaranje.png)

## 16. Approving

We click “Odobri”. Only an approved review is shown on the site and counted in the average rating search engines see. An inappropriate one is rejected.

![admin-testimoniali-odobrava](../../screenshots/testimonial-odobravanje/16-admin-testimoniali-odobrava.png)
