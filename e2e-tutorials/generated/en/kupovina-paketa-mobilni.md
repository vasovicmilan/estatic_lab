# Buying a session package (phone)

Packages have no self-checkout - an admin manually assigns one to a customer, who then spends sessions from it through the ordinary booking flow, at no extra charge. Recorded on a phone (390x844).

<video src="../../videos/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

We start on the site's home page.

![otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/01-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button. We tap it to reach the “Registracija” button.

![registracija-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/02-registracija-otvara-meni.png)

## 3. Registration

We tap “Registracija” and the account form opens.

![registracija-klik-registracija](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/03-registracija-klik-registracija.png)

## 4. Register

The customer registers first - an account has to exist before an admin can assign them a package.

![registracija](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/04-registracija.png)

## 5. Log in

The customer logs into their new account.

![prijava](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/05-prijava.png)

## 6. Home page

The administrator starts on the site's home page.

![admin-otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/06-admin-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 7. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button in the top right corner. We tap it.

![admin-login-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/07-admin-login-otvara-meni.png)

## 8. Log in

We tap “Prijava”.

![admin-login-klik-prijava](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/08-admin-login-klik-prijava.png)

## 9. Admin logs in

The admin logs in to assign a package to the customer.

![admin-prijava](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/09-admin-prijava.png)

## 10. Menu

On a phone the admin opens the main menu with the three-line button.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/10-admin-ulazak-otvara-nalog-meni.png)

## 11. Admin panel

In the “Admin” section they tap “Admin panel”.

![admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/11-admin-ulazak-otvara-admin-panel.png)

## 12. Open the "Catalog" menu

The admin panel has its own menu behind a three-line button. The admin opens it and chooses the “Catalog” group.

![admin-paketi-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/12-admin-paketi-otvara-meni.png)

## 13. Open purchased packages

The admin picks "Purchased packages" from the menu.

![admin-paketi-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/13-admin-paketi-bira-stavku.png)

## 14. Assign a new package

The admin taps "Assign package", opening the assignment form.

![admin-paketi-klik-na-dodaj](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/14-admin-paketi-klik-na-dodaj.png)

## 15. Assign the package

The admin confirms the assignment - a 3-session package is now on the customer's account, ready to use.

![admin-dodeljuje-paket](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/15-admin-dodeljuje-paket.png)

## 16. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button in the top right corner. We tap it.

![nav-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/16-nav-otvara-meni.png)

## 17. Book an appointment

We tap the “Zakažite termin” button - it leads straight to the list of services.

![nav-zakazite-termin](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/17-nav-zakazite-termin.png)

## 18. Choosing a service

In the list of services we tap the service the package covers. Its page shows the description and the variants.

![otvaranje-stranice-usluge](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/18-otvaranje-stranice-usluge.png)

## 19. Choosing a variant

We tap “Zakaži” on the variant we want and move on to choosing a time.

![izbor-varijante-usluge](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/19-izbor-varijante-usluge.png)

## 20. Choosing a date

We choose a date - the field is highlighted. The system shows the free slots for that day, based on the employees' working hours.

![izbor-datuma](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/20-izbor-datuma.png)

## 21. Choosing a time slot

We tap the time we want.

![izbor-termina](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/21-izbor-termina.png)

## 22. Paying from the package

The system recognizes the customer has a usable package for this exact service - the "pay from package" option is checked automatically, showing how many sessions remain.

![placanje-iz-paketa](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/22-placanje-iz-paketa.png)

## 23. Enter contact details

The customer enters a phone number before confirming.

![unos-kontakt-podataka](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/23-unos-kontakt-podataka.png)

## 24. Confirm the booking

The appointment is booked with no charge at all - the price is covered by the package.

![potvrda-zakazivanja](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/24-potvrda-zakazivanja.png)

## 25. Open the “Zakazivanje” menu

The admin panel has its own menu behind a three-line button. The admin opens it and chooses the “Zakazivanje” group.

![admin-termini-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/25-admin-termini-otvara-meni.png)

## 26. Open the appointment list

The admin chooses “Termini” - the list of all appointments opens.

![admin-termini-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/26-admin-termini-bira-stavku.png)

## 27. Search for the appointment

The admin searches the list by the customer's email to find the new appointment quickly.

![admin-termini-pretraga](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/27-admin-termini-pretraga.png)

## 28. Open the appointment from the results

On a phone the table scrolls sideways - the admin taps the details icon at the end of the row.

![admin-termini-otvaranje-rezultata](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/28-admin-termini-otvaranje-rezultata.png)

## 29. Admin confirms the appointment

The admin confirms the appointment - the session is now reserved from the package, but not yet spent.

![admin-potvrdjuje-termin](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/29-admin-potvrdjuje-termin.png)

## 30. Admin completes the appointment

Once the service has actually been delivered, the admin marks it completed - only now is the session from the package actually spent.

![admin-zavrsava-termin](../../screenshots/kupovina-paketa-mobilni/30-admin-zavrsava-termin.png)
