# Cancelling an appointment (phone)

A customer views and cancels their own upcoming appointment from their account, without calling or messaging the salon: account menu, “Moji termini”, the appointment from the list, “Otkaži termin”. Recorded on a phone (390x844).

<video src="../../videos/otkazivanje-termina-mobilni/merged.en.burned.mp4" controls width="720"></video>

_Subtitles are burned into the video - no external .vtt file needed_

## 1. Home page

We start on the site's home page.

![otvaranje-pocetne](../../screenshots/otkazivanje-termina-mobilni/01-otvaranje-pocetne.png)

## 2. Open the menu

On a phone the menu is hidden behind the three-line button. We tap it to reach the “Registracija” button.

![registracija-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/otkazivanje-termina-mobilni/02-registracija-otvara-meni.png)

## 3. Registration

We tap “Registracija” and the account form opens.

![registracija-klik-registracija](../../screenshots/otkazivanje-termina-mobilni/03-registracija-klik-registracija.png)

## 4. Fill in the form

We type the first name, last name, email and password (twice) and submit the form. The account is created.

![registracija](../../screenshots/otkazivanje-termina-mobilni/04-registracija.png)

## 5. Log in

After registering we are already on the login page. We type the email and password and log in.

![prijava](../../screenshots/otkazivanje-termina-mobilni/05-prijava.png)

## 6. Account menu

We tap our name (on a phone: open the menu and scroll to the section with our name).

![nalog-termini-otvara-meni](../../screenshots/otkazivanje-termina-mobilni/06-nalog-termini-otvara-meni.png)

## 7. My appointments

We choose “Moji termini”.

![nalog-termini-bira-stavku](../../screenshots/otkazivanje-termina-mobilni/07-nalog-termini-bira-stavku.png)

## 8. The appointment list

“Moji termini” shows the list of booked appointments.

![pregled-termina](../../screenshots/otkazivanje-termina-mobilni/08-pregled-termina.png)

## 9. Appointment details

We tap the appointment in the list. The cancel button is visible because the appointment is far enough in the future.

![otvaranje-detalja-termina](../../screenshots/otkazivanje-termina-mobilni/09-otvaranje-detalja-termina.png)

## 10. Cancelling

We tap “Otkaži termin”, confirm in the dialog and the appointment is cancelled.

![otkazivanje-termina](../../screenshots/otkazivanje-termina-mobilni/10-otkazivanje-termina.png)
